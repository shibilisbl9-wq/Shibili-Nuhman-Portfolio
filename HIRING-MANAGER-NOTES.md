## What gets you shortlisted

1. **A point of view in the first screen.** The cursor-builds-the-page idea is the portfolio's own case study: it shows grid discipline, motion sense and taste before I've read a word.
2. **Range with one hand visible.** Identity (DuLight, New City Light), product world (Ethra), content system (ConnectIn), campaign (Zee), film language (Ary & Maz). Different muscles, same eye.
3. **End-to-end ownership.** Concept, direction, shoot, edit, deliver. For a lean brand or in-house team that is worth two hires. Say it early, which the site does.
4. **Systems, not one-offs.** Logo variations, guidelines, templates, bilingual lockups. This is what separates a mid from a senior.
5. **Bilingual brand work (EN/AR).** Rare and valuable in this region. Currently under-sold.
